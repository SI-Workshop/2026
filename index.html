About

A working meeting for shared research directions

This workshop is organized under the HKUST-HKUST(GZ) 1+1+1 Joint Funding Program. It is intended to support cross-campus exchange around urban spatiotemporal AI, with a focus on how mobility data, spatial representations, foundation models, and reasoning methods can jointly support real-world urban systems.

01 Project exchange

Share project context, early progress, technical questions, and next milestones.

02 Research discussion

Invite related researchers to discuss methods, data, benchmarks, and applications.

03 Future collaboration

Explore joint papers, student exchange, datasets, workshops, and grant opportunities.

Project Context

Urban spatiotemporal AI for mobility and spatial reasoning

The workshop connects complementary research directions in urban informatics and computer science: modeling human mobility in real-world urban contexts, learning from spatial and trajectory data, and reasoning over maps, road networks, and location-based decision tasks.
