About

A focused workshop on spatial intelligence

The workshop creates a compact forum for exchanging ideas on spatial intelligence, including world models, urban AI, spatial data management, human mobility, and intelligent transportation.

01 Research exchange

Bring together related groups to discuss current work and emerging directions.

02 Invited perspectives

Connect academic, public-sector, and industry views on spatial data and AI.

03 Future collaboration

Explore future collaboration around datasets, methods, systems, and applications.

Project Link

Related cross-campus project

The workshop grows out of an HKUST-HKUST(GZ) cross-campus project that brings together complementary strengths in urban mobility, geospatial data, and spatially aware AI.

Agenda

Simple three-day program

Program at a glance

The workshop keeps the program intentionally simple: arrival, one full day of presentations and discussion, and a campus visit before departure.

Day 1

Arrival and informal gathering

Afternoon / Evening Arrival in Hong Kong, hotel check-in, informal welcome, and optional group dinner for early participants.
Day 2

Full-day workshop

Morning Opening remarks, invited presentations, and group discussion.
Lunch Networking lunch and informal discussion.
Afternoon Invited presentations, participant updates, and open discussion.
Late afternoon / Evening Roundtable discussion, closing remarks, and optional dinner.
Day 3

Campus visit and departure

Morning / Early afternoon HKUST campus visit, lab tour or informal group meetings, followed by departure.
